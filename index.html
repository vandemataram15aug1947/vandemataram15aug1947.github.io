Projects

Projects

Selected Projects in Power Electronics and Embedded Control, Including Multilevel and Grid-Connected PV Inverters, Advanced Modulation and Control, Motor Drives, Gate-Driver Circuits, Battery Management, Transformer Protection, and Smart-Grid Optimization.

Three-Phase Three-Level Diode-Clamped NPC Inverter

Designed and Simulated a Three-Phase, Three-Level Neutral-Point-Clamped (NPC) Voltage-Source Inverter with a Split DC Link and an LCL Output Filter. Developed Cascaded Voltage and Current PI Control in the Synchronous dq Reference Frame, Including Feedforward and Cross-Coupling Compensation. Implemented Three-Level SVPWM Sector, Region, and Dwell-Time Calculations with Twelve ePWM Gate Commands for Integration on the TMS320F28379D.

Design and Control of a Single-Phase Grid-Connected Inverter for a PV System

Designed and Simulated a Single-Stage Grid-Connected PV Inverter Using PR Control, an LCL Filter, MPPT, and Grid Synchronization. Implemented the Control Strategy on a TMS320F28379D and Evaluated Performance across a Range of Environmental, Grid, and Operating Conditions.

Field-Oriented Control of Three-Phase Induction Motor Using SVPWM Technique

Developed and Simulated Field-Oriented Control for a Three-Phase Induction Motor in MATLAB/Simulink. Implemented SVPWM on the TMS320F28379D to Achieve Accurate Speed Control, Efficient Operation, and Stable Dynamic Performance under Varying Load Conditions.

Design and Analysis of Isolated Gate Driver Circuits for Power Electronics Applications

Designed and Analyzed an Isolated Gate-Driver Circuit Based on the UCC23513DWYR for High-Frequency Power-Switching Applications. The Design Provides Galvanic Isolation, Fast Switching, and Strong Noise Immunity for Converters, Inverters, and Motor Drives.

Design and Analysis of Three-Phase Inverter Using Sinusoidal ePWM Modulation Technique

Designed and Simulated an Open-Loop Three-Phase Inverter in PLECS and MATLAB/Simulink, then Implemented the Control on a TMS320F28379D. Configured ePWM Modulation to Produce Balanced Sinusoidal Outputs and Evaluated Inverter Performance under Different Operating Conditions.

Control of DC Motor Using ePWM on the TMS320F28379D Microcontroller

Developed and Tested an ePWM-Based DC Motor Speed-Control System on the TMS320F28379D. Regulated Motor Speed by Varying the PWM Duty Cycle and Evaluated the System's Control Response and Operating Performance.

Integration of SOC and SOH Estimation for Li-Ion Battery Management

Implemented Algorithms for Estimating Battery State of Charge (SOC), State of Health (SOH), and Depth of Discharge (DOD). Integrated the Estimation Methods into a Hardware Platform for Real-Time Battery Monitoring and Analysis.

Optimal Operational Scheduling of a Grid-Connected System Using DSM

Formulated a Demand-Side Management Problem for a Grid-Connected Network Using Load Shifting. Applied a Differential Evolution Algorithm to Reduce Supply-Side Costs while Improving Demand-Side Revenue.

Overload Protection, Monitoring and Load Shifting of a 3-Phase Transformer

Developed a Real-Time System to Monitor Transformer Voltage and Current. Applied Overload Protection and Phase Load-Shifting Techniques to Improve Load Balance, Operating Efficiency, and System Reliability.
